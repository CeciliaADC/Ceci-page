The "Asteroideiak" Initiative


I'm part of Asteroideiak, a science outreach initiative we created with the idea of bringing physics and astronomy closer to people, and of keeping science alive in Basque, a language many of us sadly don't often get to use in our careers.


We're a team of ten, and every week we share outreach articles on all kinds of science topics: mostly physics and astrophysics, but also computation, biology, and mathematics.
